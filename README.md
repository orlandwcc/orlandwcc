### About Me

I am a Data Science and Cybersecurity Specialist dedicated to transforming complex datasets into strategic intelligence and engineering resilient digital environments. As the Founder of **[Orenc Data & Security Solutions](https://orencdss.vercel.app/)**, I focus on predictive analytics, AI automation, and penetration testing to build safer, smarter systems.

* **Specialization:** Predictive Analytics, AI Model Development, and Penetration Testing
* **Research Focus:** Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Vulnerability Research, and Ethical Hacking
* **Current Focus:** Deepening expertise in Advanced Machine Learning and Offensive Security
* **Collaboration:** Open to Open-Source initiatives, security consulting, and high-impact data engineering
